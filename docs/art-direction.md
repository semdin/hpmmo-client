# HPMMO Art Direction & Asset Pipeline

**Status:** Phase 2 — standards locked, candidates curated and adversarially verified, exit check captured. Standards and conventions below are the production rules from this point on; asset acceptance states live in [`assets/manifest.json`](../assets/manifest.json).
**Reference board:** [`docs/reference-board.png`](reference-board.png) (current-vs-candidate contact sheet, 12 cells). Per-asset evidence: [`docs/baseline-captures/`](baseline-captures/) (Phase 0 current state) and `tools/downloads/asset-review-*.png` (neutral + final lighting at review distances, regenerated by `scenes/test/asset_review.tscn`).

## 1. Direction statement

**Target:** grounded magical fantasy — believable stone, wood, cloth, and chitin; taller human silhouettes; warm readable interiors; clear outdoor daylight; expressive, layered magic.

**Anti-goals (do not ship):** chibi heroes, toy-like monsters, mixing realistic stone with cartoon clutter that doesn't share a lighting/material language, oversized opaque spell primitives, washed-out fog hiding unfinished geometry.

**Reference rows that must read correctly at gameplay distance (≈8 m, 65° FOV):**
| Category | Should read as | Current state (baseline) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | adult human proportions, layered robe, believable face/hands | Phase 9: hooded 1.877 m hero with 41 clips (`hero_wizard.glb`); the 2.2 m chibi is retired from the player (still used by NPCs) |
| Monster | purposeful anatomy (chitin, joints, mouthparts) | ball + bead-jointed cylinder legs (`spider_rig.gd`) |
| Stone | authored PBR masonry, correct block scale, edge wear | flat procedural brick shader, no PBR channels |
| Fire | layered translucent flame + embers + smoke | white quad particles colored at runtime |

## 2. Scale & proportions (locked standards)

- **1 unit = 1 meter** (glTF is authored in meters; Godot import scale stays **1.0**; no node rescaling except documented exception like the 0.75 Quaternius mobs).
- **Adult characters: 1.75-1.90 m** crown height, **7-7.5 head** proportions. Measure crown (not hat/accessory) from feet.
- **Phase 9 shipped hero:** `hero_wizard.glb` binds at **1.877 m** (measured in Godot), 62 joints, 6,404 tris, 5 material slots. The Phase 0 placeholder (KayKit Mage, 2.203 m crown ≈ 1.94 heads) is replaced; the retired file stays on disk because the NPCs and the character-select screen still use it.
- Character collision capsule follows the visible silhouette: **shipped r 0.35 / h 1.85**, with the camera pivot at 1.55 m, the nameplate at 2.18 m and the loot reach at 4.9 m — all derived from `player.gd`'s Phase 9 body constants, not hardcoded per call site.
- Architecture scale references: door 1.3 × 2.3 m clear; corridor 3.0 m clear; story height 4.0 m; stair rise/run 0.18/0.28 m (grand staircase 0.15/0.35, landings every 12 steps); railing 1.0 m; broom flight clearance ≥ 2.5 m above outdoor paths.
- **Camera clearance:** the third-person spring arm is 8.0 m (wheel clamp 3-16 m, r 0.45 capsule). Interior rooms and corridors must allow ≥ 3 m of camera pull-back along the movement axis without clipping into geometry that reads as a wall; place colliders on all intended occluders so the spring arm resolves cleanly.
- KayKit modular grid (already in the kit): 4 m primary wall tile, 2 m half pieces, 1 m thick blocks — new kit pieces must snap to this grid until Phase 10 replaces it wholesale.

## 3. Conventions

- **Forward axis: +Z** for characters and vehicles (the shipped rigs and the yaw math `atan2(x, z)` both assume it). Godot cameras look down local −Z; do not "fix" model yaw outside the model wrapper.
- **Up: +Y.** Applied transforms: no un-applied scale, **no negative scales** (mirror by geometry), rotations applied in the source file.
- **Root motion: none.** All locomotion is code-driven; author clips in place (the Quaternius UAL pack ships a separate `_RM` root-motion variant — use the in-place `Standard` GLBs; convert any `_RM` clip before adoption).
- **Rig naming (Phase 9, shipped):** sockets are `BoneAttachment3D` nodes named `Socket_Wand`, `Socket_Hand_L/R`, `Socket_Foot_L/R`, `Socket_Torso`, `Socket_Head`, `Socket_Hips` on the hero skeleton, and the broom exports `MountRoot`, `SeatSocket`, `GripSocket`, `TailSocket`. One convention, documented in [`phase9-rig-and-sockets.md`](phase9-rig-and-sockets.md). The retired KayKit rig is still used by NPCs and the character-select screen.
- **Animation clip naming:** `<Action>_<Variant>` (e.g. `Spellcast_Shoot`, `Running_A`); one-shot vs loop is authored as loop mode in the clip, never left to callers (the current player never sets loop modes — fixed alongside Phase 9 clips).
- **Animation events:** method tracks named `fx:<effect_id>` / `sfx:<sound_id>` / `footstep:<surface>` at contact frames — Phase 9/12 consume these.
- **UVs:** 0-1, no overlaps except explicitly mirrored islands; architecture kits share trim-sheet UV space.
- **Texture naming:** `<asset>_<channel>_<res>.<ext>` (e.g. `ashlar_albedo_2k.png`); channels: `albedo` (sRGB), `normal`, `rough`, `ao`, `height`, `orm`. Masks/normals/flow are linear data — never import them as sRGB.
- **Material slots:** ≤ 2 per ordinary asset, ≤ 4 per hero (current hero: multiple; audit before promotion).

## 4. Budgets (ceilings, not targets)

| Asset | LOD0 ceiling | Texture | Measured baseline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | 25-45k tris | 2k, ≤ 4 slots | Phase 9 hero: 6,404 tris / 62 joints / 41 clips / 5 slots (textures still flat constants; LODs outstanding) |
| Ordinary monster | 8-20k tris | 1-2k | 5.9-7.4k tris / 43 joints / 14 clips (Quaternius) |
| Boss | 25-50k tris | 2k | commander = reused Orc_Skull at 1.65× visual scale |
| Architecture module | 0.2-3k tris/module, shared trim sheets | 2k sets, 256 px/m texel density (±15%) | 220-2,010 tris/module (KayKit) ✓ |
| Particle elements | 256-512 px source, 1-2k atlases | — | 2 stills 512² (Kenney) |
| Audio | WAV masters, compressed playback | — | **zero audio files** |

Low-poly stylized is acceptable where consistent **within a material family**; the failure mode is mixing fidelity levels in one frame (see `tools/downloads/asset-review-actors-neutral.png`). Do not inflate tri counts to hit a ceiling.

**LOD and collision standard (applies from Phase 9 onward):**
- LODs: hero and bosses ship LOD1 (~50-60% tris) and LOD2 (~25-30%) with `visibility_range` set by measurement; ordinary mobs LOD1 only; architecture uses occlusion-friendly modules rather than per-mesh LODs; props ≤ 1k tris need none. Every manifest entry's `lods` field is updated to the real state at integration — `none` is the pre-integration value, not an accepted end state.
- Collision: authored proxies, never render meshes — a capsule per character (updated to the §2 silhouette), convex/box proxies per architecture module and prop, sphere joints for creature limbs where needed. Server collision/navigation exports are versioned content artifacts (Phase 3/8) and are excluded from client release packaging.
- Current assets ship with generated/scene collision only (KayKit modules have none); Phase 8's greybox owns the first authored export.

## 5. Materials & PBR policy

- Stone/wood/cloth/chitin are **non-metallic**; metallic maps only where physically justified (armor hardware).
- Every static architectural surface ships albedo + normal + roughness (AO where useful); no flat-color geometry for hero-path surfaces.
- Normal maps import as Normal Map (Godot auto-detect, verify), OpenGL (+Y) convention — state the convention in the manifest when it differs.
- Roughness texture drives the red channel; AO via `ao_texture`. Channel-packed ORM only where the source provides it.
- No lighting baked into albedo (the current 256² castle texture set does exactly this — dead assets, replaced in Phase 10).

## 6. Acceptance states (manifest vocabulary)

`missing` → nothing exists. `candidate` → downloaded, license-recorded, pending review. `placeholder` → shipped but explicitly not final art. `integrated` → in-game and referenced by scenes. `accepted` → passed the review scene at gameplay distance **and** the exit checks of its owning phase (a screenshot alone never approves rigging or animation).

## 7. Pipeline & storage

- **Import pass required after adding files:** `godot --headless --editor --import --quit`, then run scenes windowed.
- **Review instrument:** `scenes/test/asset_review.tscn` — two rows (actors at z 0, surfaces at z −12) shot from ~9.9 m / ~10.5 m (the 6-figure actor spread and the 4 m wall pair need slightly more than the 8 m spring-arm length; close-ups at 3.6 m), neutral + final lighting, PNGs to `tools/downloads/asset-review-*.png`. Run: `godot --path . res://scenes/test/asset_review.tscn` (import pass first for new files).
- **Audit tool:** `python tools/asset_audit.py` → `tools/downloads/asset-audit.json` (tris, joints, clips, bbox, sha256 for every GLB/GLTF; PNG dimensions/color type). The manifest's measured fields come from this, never from memory.
- **Folder layout:** review-stage files under `assets/candidates/<category>/<id>/` (imported, reviewable, excluded from release packaging); promoted files move to `assets/models|textures|audio/<family>/` with the manifest updated and the candidate copy deleted.
- **Provenance:** every manifest entry records source URL, author, license + license URL + local license text copy; keep the license file beside the asset.
- **Git LFS rules (defined now, activated with the Phase 3 repo split):**
  ```
  *.glb *.gltf *.bin *.blend *.fbx *.wav *.ogg *.mp3 filter=lfs diff=lfs merge=lfs -text
  assets/**/*.png assets/**/*.jpg filter=lfs diff=lfs merge=lfs -text
  ```
  Current tree: LFS installed but unconfigured; no LFS pointers exist yet.
- **Release packaging must exclude:** `assets/candidates/`, `.blend`/source art, `docs/previews`, `tools/downloads`, and any server collision/navigation packages (those are versioned server-side content artifacts).

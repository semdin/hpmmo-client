spell effects source art - provenance (updated 2026-10-16)

GENERATED (spell flipbooks and stills):
  atlases/fire_burst, flame_loop, smoke_puff, explosion, burst_gold, burst_red,
  burst_crimson, burst_green, ripple_blue, shockwave, wand_flash, spark_burst,
  energy_orb, fire_orb (8x8 flipbooks) were generated with fal.ai Wan 3.0
  text-to-video (alibaba/wan-3.0/text-to-video) on a pure black background;
  tex/rune_seal, star_flare, lightning_strands were generated with fal.ai
  Z-Image Turbo (fal-ai/z-image/turbo). Prompts and the raw clips/stills are in
  art/vfx/ (README.md, source/). client/tools/vfx/build_spell_vfx.py keys the
  black background to alpha, packs the frames (4 px zero gutter, straight alpha,
  row-major) and registers them in metadata.json. Generated output; commercial
  use is permitted under the fal.ai terms of the generating account. No
  third-party image or effect pack was used as an input.

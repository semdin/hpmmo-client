feast.glb and its extracted feast_feast_*.webp textures contain 3D models from
NVIDIA SimReady Assets (https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/simready-assets),
Copyright NVIDIA Corporation, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the models were decimated, their normal maps re-baked, their textures
shrunk into one shared atlas, and they were arranged into table settings for the
castle's Great Hall and kitchens. The gold plates, platters and goblets are
original geometry made for this project. The full list of packages used is in
assets/ASSET_CREDITS.md.
